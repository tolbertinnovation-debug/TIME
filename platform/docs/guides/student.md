# Guide for students

## 1. Start

1. Open the platform and choose **Create account**. Use your real name: it is printed on your certificate.
2. Choose **Apply**, pick your class, enter your county and choose **Send application**.
3. The registrar approves or rejects it. You get a notification either way. On approval you receive a student number (for example TIME-2027-0001).

## 2. Pay your fees

Your class opens when Finance confirms your first payment.

1. Choose **Fees**. **How to pay** shows TIME's Mobile Money and bank details.
2. Pay by Mobile Money or bank transfer, then enter the amount and the reference of your payment and choose **Send to Finance**.
3. Finance checks it against TIME's records and confirms it. You then get a receipt you can open and print.

The balance is due **8 weeks after your first payment**. If it is not paid by then, your class is paused until you pay. If you need more time, open **Ask for more time to pay** on the Fees page before the due date. Finance decides and you are told the answer.

## 3. Study

- **My classes**, then **Open class**, shows your progress, lessons, assessments, announcements and coming sessions.
- Open a lesson, study it, then choose **Mark lesson complete**.
- **Practical work** is signed off by your lecturer or study centre facilitator, not by you.
- **Class discussion** is for questions about the class. Your lecturer and classmates can reply.

### Studying with no connection

- On the class page choose **Save lessons for offline** while you have a connection. Keep the page open until it says the lessons are saved.
- Any lesson you have opened before is also kept.
- With no connection you can **read** saved lessons. Marking a lesson complete, quizzes, handing in work, fees and messages need a connection.
- Saved lessons are removed from the phone when you sign out. On a shared phone, always sign out.

## 4. Assessments and results

- Your mark is out of 100: continuous assessment 30, assignments and practical activities 20, midterm 20, final 30. The pass mark is **70**.
- Written work: type your answer and hand it in before the due date. After the due date it can no longer be handed in.
- Quizzes: choose **Start the quiz**. If the quiz has a time limit, the clock starts then and does not stop. Choose **Hand in quiz** before time runs out: a quiz handed in after the time limit receives no marks. If your connection failed, tell your lecturer, who can let you take it again.
- Your result appears on the class page once academic leadership has approved it. If you believe it is wrong, open **Appeal this result** and explain why.

## 5. Your certificate

1. When TIME has ended the class and your result is published, choose **Certificates**. The page shows a checklist of what is needed: a pass, your fees paid in full, and the class ended.
2. When every item is ticked, choose **Request my certificate**.
3. The registrar checks your record, then academic leadership approves the certificate.
4. Open it from **Certificates** to view or print it. Anyone can confirm it is real at **Check a certificate** using the number and code printed on it.
5. **My transcript**, also on the Certificates page, lists every class you have taken with your published results. Print it yourself, or ask the Registrar to sign and stamp it for an official copy.

## 6. Other things

- **Withdraw or defer:** on **My classes**, open **Ask to withdraw or defer**. The registrar decides.
- **Sponsored students:** your sponsor receives a summary of your progress only if you choose **I agree to share my progress**. You can withdraw this at any time. Your individual work, feedback, messages and fees are never shared.
- **Help:** choose **Help**, pick what it is about and write your question.
