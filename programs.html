Why study with TIME

From subject expert to professional educator

Many people know their subject well but have never been trained to teach it. We develop the teaching skills, professional ethics, classroom management, assessment practices and leadership that turn subject knowledge into effective learning.

MoE accredited

Supplementary teacher-training institution under the Bureau of Teacher Education.

10-week courses

Professional programs run for 10 weeks, with weekday or Saturday classes.

Affordable

Teacher certificate fees can be paid in two installments.

Margibi campus

Classes at the Phìtìa Academy campus, Robertsfield Highway.
