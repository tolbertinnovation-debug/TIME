Focus areas

What we work on

01

Preventing Educational Poverty

Improving learning environments by equipping schools with textbooks, copybooks, chalkboards, benches and other essentials.

02

Teacher Professional Development

Accredited training that gives teachers the pedagogy, ethics and classroom skills their learners deserve.

03

Girls' Education & Women's Empowerment

Creating opportunities for girls, women and widows, offering practical support and helping protect young women from exploitation.

04

Child Protection & Health

Raising awareness of child protection and safeguarding, and supporting community health campaigns that protect vulnerable children.

05

Orphans & Vulnerable Children

Care, schooling and belonging for orphans and children from struggling households, including through Phìtìa Academy.

06

Climate & Child Rights

Addressing environmental threats to children's well-being, supported by training in environmental health and GIS mapping.

Success stories

Inspiring stories from the communities we've helped

523Children · 6 schools
Grand Bassa County

Reaching 523 underserved children across six remote schools

We identified and connected with 523 underserved children scattered across six remote schools in Grand Bassa County. These young learners, once overlooked, are now at the center of our commitment to quality education, empowering their futures and breaking the chains of educational poverty.

122Students supported
Kpehpue Town, Grand Bassa

Equipping 122 eager students at Kpehpue Town Community School

Learners who had faced real barriers to education now have the tools and resources they need to thrive. Through our programs we have offered them not only the gift of knowledge but a renewed sense of hope.

2026Graduating class
Margibi campus

A new class of trained professionals

The TIME Inc. Class of 2026 walked across the stage in caps, gowns and green-and-red stoles. They are teachers and professionals now better equipped to serve their schools, churches and communities.
