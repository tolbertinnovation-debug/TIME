Our books

Dr. Philip V. Saywrayne, III, Executive Director of TIME Inc., has authored 12 books, along with peer-reviewed journal articles.

Mastering School Leadership

Inspiring Growth, Building Community and Driving Excellence

An essential resource for educators and administrators who want to build leadership skills and foster a thriving school environment, with practical strategies, real-world insights and proven best practices.

It covers managing people, strengthening school culture, improving student outcomes, instructional leadership, school climate, teacher development, community engagement and the innovative use of technology in education. For current and aspiring school leaders, teachers stepping into leadership roles, and consultants supporting schools.
