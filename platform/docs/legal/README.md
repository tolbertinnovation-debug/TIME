# Terms and privacy: drafts for TIME's review

**Status: draft. Not yet shown to students and not yet approved by TIME.**

| Document | What it is |
|---|---|
| [TERMS.md](TERMS.md) | The rules a person agrees to when they create an account |
| [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) | What TIME collects about people, why, who sees it and how long it is kept |

These drafts describe what the platform actually does today. They were written by the platform's developer, not by a lawyer, and they are not legal advice. Before they are used:

1. **Fill in every item in [square brackets].** Each is a decision only TIME can make. They are listed below.
2. **Have them reviewed** by someone with authority at TIME and, ideally, by a lawyer in Liberia.
3. **Tell the developer they are approved.** Acceptance is then added to the sign-up page, and the date each person agreed is recorded.

## Decisions TIME needs to make

| # | Decision | Where | Suggested |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contact email and phone for questions about accounts and personal information | Both | A shared office address, not one person's |
| 2 | Minimum age to hold an account, and what is needed below it | Terms 2 | 18, or younger with a parent or guardian's written consent |
| 3 | Refund rules: when fees are returned, and how much | Terms 6 | TIME's existing refund policy, word for word |
| 4 | How long a result appeal can be made after results are published | Terms 7 | 14 days |
| 5 | How long academic records and certificates are kept | Privacy 7 | Permanently, so certificates can always be verified |
| 6 | How long payment records are kept | Privacy 7 | 7 years |
| 7 | How long the activity log is kept | Privacy 7 | 3 years |
| 8 | How long an unused account (never enrolled) is kept | Privacy 7 | 2 years after the last sign-in |
| 9 | How many days TIME takes to answer a request about personal information | Privacy 8 | 30 days |
| 10 | The date these take effect | Both | The first day of the pilot |

## When the platform changes

The privacy notice must be updated before any of these is switched on, because each sends personal information to a new company: email delivery, SMS or WhatsApp delivery, file storage for uploaded work. The notice already names them as "planned".
