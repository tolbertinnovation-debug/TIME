Your information

Privacy policy

How TIME Inc. handles the information you share through this website, our contact form and TIME Learning.

On this pageIn shortVisiting the websiteThe contact formWhatsApp, phone and emailTIME Learning accountsWho we share information with

In short

  • The public website uses no cookies, no advertising and no tracking or analytics tools.
  • We only receive personal information that you choose to send us: through the contact form, by WhatsApp, phone or email, or by creating a TIME Learning account.
  • We use it to answer you, to run our courses and to keep records of your learning. We do not sell it or share it for marketing.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

Visiting the website

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub automatically receives technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you load a page, and may keep it in security logs. We cannot see these logs and do not use them.

Fonts and images are served from the website itself, so loading a page does not contact Google or other third parties. Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps and other sites only connect you to those services when you choose to follow them; their own privacy policies then apply.

The contact form

When you send a message through our contact form, we receive your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number (if you give it), the topic you choose and your message.

The form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-forwarding service, to the TIME Inc. office inbox at tinstitute312@gmail.com, which is a Google Gmail account. We use your details only to reply to you and to follow up on your request, such as a course registration, donation or partnership.

If the form cannot send, the website offers to open your own email app or WhatsApp with your message instead. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it there.

WhatsApp, phone and email

If you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, we receive your number or email address and whatever you tell us. WhatsApp messages are handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own terms. We use these conversations only to respond to you and to provide the service you asked about.

TIME Learning accounts

If you create an account on TIME Learning, we store the information needed to run your courses:

  • Account details: your name and email address. Passwords and sign-in are handled by Neon Auth, our account provider; we never see your password. A one-time code is emailed to you to confirm your address.
  • Course records: course requests and approvals, your cohort, lessons you mark complete, quiz and test answers and results, written submissions and any files you upload, grades and lecturer feedback, and class attendance recorded by staff.
  • Support requests: the questions you send through the support centre and the replies.
  • Activity records: a log of important account and teaching actions (for example approvals and grade changes), kept for security and to resolve disputes. Message contents are not included in this log.

This information is stored in a private database run by Neon on Amazon Web Services servers in Frankfurt, Germany. It is visible only to you, to the lecturers assigned to your courses (for those courses), and to TIME administrators. Administrators can export course reports for TIME staff use only.

The free study guides on TIME Learning save your notes and progress in your own browser on your device. They are not sent to us unless you choose to save a copy to your account.

Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. We only share it:

  • with the service providers named above (GitHub, FormSubmit, Google Gmail, Neon and its email delivery, WhatsApp), only so they can provide their service to us;
  • with the Ministry of Education or other authorities where the law requires it, for example for accredited teacher-training records;
  • with your permission, for example to confirm your certificate to an employer you name.

How long we keep it

We keep contact messages for as long as we need them to answer and follow up on your request. We keep learning records for as long as needed to provide your course and to confirm your training and results afterwards. You can ask us to delete information we no longer need.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see a copy of, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you. Contact us using the details below. We will confirm your identity before acting on a request, and we will tell you if we must keep some records, for example results that confirm a certificate.

Children

This website and TIME Learning are intended for adults: teachers, professionals, parents, donors and partners. Information about pupils of Phìtìa Academy is handled by the school directly and is not collected through this website.

Security

The website and TIME Learning use encrypted connections (HTTPS). TIME Learning checks every request against your verified account, and lecturers can only reach the courses assigned to them. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not upload medical records or other highly sensitive documents.
