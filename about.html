Our story

It is a story about people.

What started with a teacher listening to his students grew into an organization serving teachers, children, women and communities across Liberia.

It began with listening

As a classroom teacher, Dr. Philip V. Saywrayne, III began noticing things that concerned him deeply. Many students, particularly young women, struggled to keep up. Some submitted assignments late or not at all. Some slept during lectures. Some young men left campus before the school day had ended.

These behaviors could easily have been read as a lack of seriousness. He believed there was more to the story. He began listening and later administered a diagnostic assessment to understand what was really happening.

The answers were painful but important. Students were struggling to pay school fees, buy basic materials and meet daily needs. Some worked long, difficult hours just to remain in school. Some young women had become vulnerable to exploitative survival practices because of economic hardship; some young men were exposed to theft, substances and other risks.

Sometimes a student does not need another reprimand. Sometimes the student needs someone to listen, someone to care, and someone to help create a pathway through the difficulty.

He and his wife began helping wherever they could. There was no organization, large budget or elaborate structure. They saw needs and responded with whatever they had: school supplies, encouragement and practical support, offered quietly and personally.

From personal kindness to an institution

As they heard more stories from young people, women, families and communities, it became clear that the need was far greater than two individuals could meet. The need also extended beyond the capital. Rural towns and villages had children without basic materials, teachers without professional support, and women and widows in need.

That raised a simple question: if the needs are so great, and we are already trying to help, should there not be an organization through which this work can be organized, strengthened and continued?

In January 2021, the Technical Institute for Many Empowerment (TIME Inc.) was established and registered with the Government of Liberia. Dr. Saywrayne, his partner Ms. Tibeh E. Gbassagee-Saywrayne, and their daughter Ms. Tijue N. Suah developed the organization together and coined its name, a name for an institution that would not merely respond to immediate needs, but also empower people through education, professional development, skills training, humanitarian assistance and self-reliance.

Our guiding Scripture

“Do not withhold good from those to whom it is due, when it is in your power to act.”

Proverbs 3:27 (NIV). We may not be able to solve every problem, but we can do something. And sometimes what seems small to us means a great deal to someone who has very little.

Walking to where the need is

The first organization to provide significant encouragement and support was WE-CARE Liberia. Through this partnership, TIME Inc. extended its outreach to underprivileged women, children, teachers and communities in rural and hard-to-reach areas of Grand Bassa, Bong and Margibi Counties.

We found children attending school without textbooks, copybooks or pencils, and schools with damaged chalkboards and too few benches. Improving education, we learned, sometimes requires physically showing up. We have walked two to three hours to reach remote villages, carrying textbooks, copybooks, pencils and stationery; we have replaced damaged chalkboards and repaired benches.

Much of this has been made possible through our own limited resources and proceeds from the sale of books and educational materials. We have sometimes given from what we ourselves had, because we could not stand before a need and simply say, “We do not have enough.”

Developing professional teachers

As TIME Inc. grew, its work expanded into structured educational and professional development. We are now an accredited supplementary teacher-training institution under the Bureau of Teacher Education, Ministry of Education.

Many people possess substantial knowledge in a subject but have had little formal preparation in pedagogy. Our goal is to help subject specialists become skilled professional educators with a strong foundation in classroom management, lesson planning, instructional methods, assessment, educational leadership and professional ethics.

Our purpose is not simply to issue certificates. It is to develop teachers who know what they are doing, understand why they are doing it and can make a meaningful difference in the lives of their learners.

What we believe

Educational problems rarely exist in isolation. A child who cannot afford a schoolbook may struggle academically. A student working long hours to pay fees arrives exhausted. A teacher who never received adequate preparation struggles to give learners the instruction they deserve. So we address education from a broader perspective.

Our Vision

A Liberia where every person, whatever their circumstances, can develop their God-given potential through education and opportunity.

Our Mission

To empower people through education, serve humanity with compassion, strengthen teachers through professional development, support vulnerable women and children, and extend learning to underserved communities.

Our Values

Dignity, compassion, sacrifice, integrity and service, with humanity at the center of everything we do.
