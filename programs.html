Liberian dollar amounts use the approved rate of US$1 = LRD 170. If the rate changes, the official rate in effect when you pay applies. For Special Education Support Training (Cohort #1), ask admissions.

Other fees

  • Registration/application fee: US$15, where applicable, in addition to tuition.
  • Exit examination fee: US$10 (LRD 1,700), paid before the examination date. It is separate from tuition.
  • Tuition covers instruction only unless stated in writing. Admissions will tell you before you enroll whether learning materials, exams, certificate processing or internship costs are extra.

50% tuition discount

TIME Inc. may grant a 50% discount on tuition, subject to eligibility and approval, to:

  • Eligible staff of Phìtìa Academy (PHICA)
  • Approved groups enrolling together
  • Students who meet the early-payment conditions

The discount covers tuition only, not registration, exam, materials or certificate charges. Confirm your eligibility before registering.

Payment rules

  • Pay only through official TIME Inc. payment channels, and keep every official receipt.
  • All tuition must be paid, and financial clearance obtained, before the exit examination.
  • Certificates and diplomas are issued only when all academic, attendance, exam and payment requirements are met.

Why study with TIME

From subject expert to professional educator

Many people know their subject well but have never been trained to teach it. We develop the teaching skills, professional ethics, classroom management, assessment practices and leadership that turn subject knowledge into effective learning.

MoE accredited

Supplementary teacher-training institution under the Bureau of Teacher Education.

10-week courses

Professional certificate and diploma courses run for 10 weeks, with weekday or Saturday classes.

Affordable

The “C” Certificate costs US$80, payable in two installments. See all fees.
