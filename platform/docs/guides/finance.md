# Guide for the finance officer

You work from **Finance** in the menu. Approvals and the help desk are under **Administration**.

## Set up once

**Payment details:** enter TIME's Mobile Money number and name, and the bank account details. Students see exactly what you enter here under **How to pay**. Check every digit.

## Each day

1. Open **Payments**. Each entry is a payment a student says they have made, with the amount and the reference.
2. Find the same reference in the Mobile Money statement or the bank statement.
3. If it is there and the amount matches, choose **Confirm payment**. The student gets a numbered receipt and, if it is their first payment, their class opens.
4. If it is not there, choose **Reject** and say why. The student is told and can send it again.

**Never confirm a payment you have not seen in TIME's own records.** A reference can be used only once; the platform refuses a repeat.

## Balances

- **Student balances** lists every student with what they were charged, what they have paid and what they owe. Search by name or student number.
- Open a student to **Add charge** (for example a resit fee) or **Record payment** for money received directly at the office.

## The eight-week rule

- The balance is due 8 weeks after the student's first confirmed payment.
- Students see a warning 14 days before the due date.
- After the due date the class is paused for that student until the balance is paid. Confirming the payment restores access at once.

## Payment arrangements

A student who needs more time sends a request, which arrives in **Approvals**. If you approve it, access continues until the date agreed. If that date passes unpaid, the class is paused.

## Refunds and cancelled balances

Money only leaves TIME with two people's agreement: you ask, academic leadership approves.

1. Open the student under **Student balances**. At the bottom, under **Refunds**, enter the amount to refund and the reason. Tick **Also cancel the ... still to pay** if the student has withdrawn and should not owe the rest. You can cancel the balance without a refund by leaving the amount empty.
2. You cannot refund more than the student has paid and not had back, and only one request per student can wait at a time.
3. Academic leadership approves or rejects it in **Approvals**. You are notified either way. A cancelled balance takes effect at once.
4. An approved refund appears under **Finance**, **Refunds**, **Approved, to send**. Send the money by Mobile Money or bank transfer, then enter the transaction reference and choose **Record as sent**. The student is notified, and only then does the refund come off what they have paid.

The **Refunds** report lists every approved refund, who approved and who sent it.

## Reports

Under **Administration**, **Reports**, download the **Payments** report: every confirmed payment with its receipt number, date, student, amount, method and reference, for one intake or all of them. Use it to reconcile against the Mobile Money and bank statements.

## Help desk

Requests about fees and payments come to you under **Administration**, **Help desk**.

## What you cannot do

See students' work or marks, change roles, or approve results and certificates.
