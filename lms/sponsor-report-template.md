# Sponsor progress report: consent and template

Policy decided 31 Oct 2026. Sponsors receive **periodic, summarized** progress reports on the students they sponsor, only with the student's **informed consent**. Reports go out at the end of each term or at an agreed interval. The Registrar prepares each report, the Executive Director (or a delegate) approves it, and then it is sent.

---

## Part A — Student consent form

*Give the student a copy. Keep the signed original on file and tick `sponsor_report_consent` in their Moodle profile.*

> **Consent to share academic progress with my sponsor**
>
> I, ______________________ (student name), student ID ________, agree that the Technical Institute for Many Empowerment (TIME) Inc. may share a **summary** of my academic progress with my sponsor, ______________________, at the end of each term or at an agreed interval, for as long as they sponsor my studies.
>
> The summary may include: my enrolment status; my attendance or participation; whether I completed required assignments; my assessment results or overall grades; my academic standing; and whether I completed my program.
>
> TIME Inc. will **not** share my individual assignments, detailed academic records, personal messages or other confidential information without my further written permission.
>
> I understand that I can withdraw this consent at any time by writing to the Registrar. Withdrawing consent will not affect my studies, but it may affect the sponsorship arrangement.
>
> Student signature: ______________________ Date: ____________
>
> Registrar: ______________________ Date: ____________

## Where each figure comes from in Moodle

| Report item | Moodle source |
|---|---|
| Enrolment status | Course → Participants (Active/Suspended) |
| Attendance & participation | Attendance activity report; "Continuous assessment" category total out of 30 |
| Assignments completed | Activity completion report |
| Overall grade | Gradebook → course total (out of 100) |
| Academic standing | Registrar's record, based on the pass mark and policy |
| Completion | Course completion report; Custom certificate code |

**Never include:** submitted work, instructor feedback on individual pieces of work, messages or forum posts, health, family or financial details, or the student's contact information.
