# TIME Learning: Privacy Notice

**DRAFT for TIME's review. Not yet in force.** Items in [square brackets] are for TIME to decide.

Effective from: [date]

This notice explains what the Technical Institute for Many Empowerment (TIME) Inc. ("TIME", "we") collects about you on TIME Learning, why, who can see it and how long it is kept. TIME is responsible for this information.

## 1. In short

- We collect only what is needed to teach you, assess you, record your fees and issue your certificate.
- We do not sell your information, and the platform carries no advertising and no advertising trackers.
- Your sponsor sees a summary of your progress only if you agree.
- Staff see only what their job needs, and everything staff do is recorded.

## 2. What we collect

| Information | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| **Account:** your name, email address, and your password in a scrambled form that nobody can read | You, when you create an account |
| **Application and enrolment:** your county, the class you applied to, your student number, and any request to withdraw or defer | You and the Registrar |
| **Learning:** lessons you completed, practical work signed off and by whom, attendance at sessions | You, your lecturer and your facilitator |
| **Assessment:** your answers and work handed in, marks, feedback, results and any appeal | You and your lecturer |
| **Fees:** what you were charged, payments you reported with their amount, method and reference, receipts, and any request for more time to pay | You and the Finance Office |
| **Certificates:** the certificate issued to you, its number and date, and whether it has been revoked | TIME |
| **Messages:** your posts in class discussions, your requests to the help desk and the replies | You and TIME staff |
| **Course evaluations:** your ratings and comments about a course. These are stored without your name or any link to you; TIME records only that you handed one in | You |
| **Sponsorship:** who sponsors you, and whether and when you agreed to share your progress | The Registrar and you |
| **Activity record:** what you did on the platform and when, for example signing in, handing in work or reporting a payment | Recorded automatically |
| **Technical:** the internet address and the type of browser used each time you sign in | Recorded automatically |

We do not collect your Mobile Money PIN, bank card numbers or bank passwords. You pay through your own Mobile Money or bank account, not through the platform.

## 3. Why we use it

- To create your account and keep it secure.
- To decide your application and enrol you.
- To teach you and record your progress and attendance.
- To assess your work and publish your results.
- To record your fees and payments and give you receipts.
- To issue your certificate and let others confirm it is genuine.
- To answer your requests for help.
- To report to the Ministry of Education and other authorities where TIME is required to.
- To keep the platform secure and to investigate misuse, using the activity record.

## 4. Who can see it

Within TIME, each role sees only what it needs:

| Role | Can see |
|---|---|
| Your lecturer and study centre facilitator | Your name, progress, attendance and work in the classes they teach. Not your fees. |
| Registrar | Your application, enrolment, results and certificates. |
| Finance Office | Your charges, payments and balance. Not your work or marks. |
| Academic leadership | Results, certificates, lessons under review, and the activity record. |
| System administrators | Everything, in order to run the platform and help when something goes wrong. |
| Your classmates | Your name on posts you make in the class discussion. |

Outside TIME:

- **The public** can check a certificate by its number and code. They see the holder's name, the award and course, its date and whether it is valid. Nothing else.
- **Your sponsor**, only if you have agreed on the platform, receives a summary: enrolment status, attendance, whether assignments were completed, overall result, academic standing and completion. Your sponsor never receives your individual assignments, detailed marks or feedback, your messages, or your fee records. You can withdraw your agreement at any time on the **My classes** page; reports produced afterwards will not include you.
- **The Ministry of Education and other authorities**, where the law or accreditation requires TIME to report.
- **Companies that run the platform for TIME.** They store or carry the information on TIME's instructions and may not use it for themselves:
  - Vercel (United States): runs the platform.
  - Neon (database, held in [region]): stores the records.
  - Planned, and to be named here before they are used: an email delivery service, an SMS or WhatsApp delivery service, and a file storage service.

This means your information is stored on computers outside Liberia.

## 5. Cookies and your phone

- The platform uses one cookie, to keep you signed in. It is not used to track you elsewhere.
- If you install the platform on your phone, or use **Save lessons for offline**, copies of your class and lesson pages are kept on that phone so you can read them with no connection. They are removed when you sign out. On a shared phone, always sign out.

## 6. How we protect it

- All traffic between your phone and the platform is encrypted (HTTPS).
- Passwords are stored only in a scrambled form. Sign-in locks for 10 minutes after 5 wrong passwords.
- Every page checks that the person signed in is allowed to see it.
- Every action by staff and students is written to an activity record that academic leadership and administrators can review.
- Decisions that matter, such as enrolment, results, payment arrangements and certificates, need approval by the responsible office.

No system is perfectly secure. If your information is exposed in a way that could harm you, TIME will tell you and say what it is doing about it.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You may:

- see most of your information yourself on the platform: your classes, progress, results, fees, receipts and certificates;
- ask TIME for a copy of the information it holds about you;
- ask TIME to correct information that is wrong, including the spelling of your name before a certificate is issued;
- ask TIME to close your account and delete information it does not need to keep. Records listed in section 7 are kept for the periods shown;
- agree, or withdraw your agreement, to share your progress with a sponsor.

To do any of these, use **Help** on the platform or contact TIME at [email address] or [phone number]. TIME will answer within [30 days].
