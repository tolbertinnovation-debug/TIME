Admissions & programs

Is TIME Inc. accredited?

Yes. TIME Inc. is an accredited supplementary teacher-training institution under the Bureau of Teacher Education, Ministry of Education (MoE), Liberia. It was established and registered with the Government of Liberia in January 2021.

What programs do you offer?

We offer the “C” Certificate in Elementary Education, “C” Certificate lab training for Senior High science teachers, other skills courses such as English and research skills, 11 professional certificate programs and 10 diploma programs. See the full list on the Programs page.

How long are the courses?

The “C” Certificate in Elementary Education runs for nine months. Professional certificate and diploma courses run for 10 weeks each. The laboratory training for Senior High science teachers is 10 weeks plus a two-month supervised internship. Skills course durations are confirmed for each course. See all programs and fees.

When are classes held?

Depending on the program, enrolment and instructors, classes run on weekday afternoons, on Saturdays, or both. Typical times are:

  • Weekday track: Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM
  • Saturday track: Saturday only, 8:00 AM – 3:00 PM

The administration gives registered students their exact timetable before classes begin.

How do I register?

Registration is ongoing. Visit our campus Monday – Friday, 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM, call an admissions line (+231 880 902 568, +231 776 037 641, +231 777 714 784 or +231 778 138 548), message us on WhatsApp, or send us a message.

How much do the courses cost?

Approved tuition fees are:

  • “C” Certificate in Elementary Education: US$80 (LRD 13,600)
  • Professional certificate courses: US$125 per course
  • Diploma courses: US$175 per course
  • Skills courses (Standard English, Research Skills, Records Keeping): US$125 per course
  • Laboratory training for Senior High science teachers: confirmed by the administration before registration

Liberian dollar amounts use the approved rate of US$1 = LRD 170. Full fee details.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes, for the “C” Certificate in Elementary Education: two equal installments of US$40 (LRD 6,800), the first when you register and the second before the exit examination. For other courses, admissions will give you the approved payment schedule. All tuition must be paid before the exit examination.

When does the next class start?

For the 2027 intake, the registration deadline is Monday, January 4, 2027 and classes begin on Monday, March 8, 2027. The following intake is on Thursday, October 14, 2027. These dates apply unless TIME Inc. issues an official written notice changing them.

Are there other fees besides tuition?

A registration/application fee of US$15 applies where applicable, in addition to tuition. The exit examination fee is US$10 (LRD 1,700), paid before the examination date and separate from tuition. You must also have paid all tuition and received financial clearance before you sit the exit examination.

What does the tuition fee cover?

Unless stated in writing, tuition covers instruction only. Before you enroll, admissions will tell you whether learning materials, assessments and exams, certificate or diploma processing, internship costs or other charges are included or charged separately.

Are discounts or scholarships available?

TIME Inc. may grant a 50% tuition discount, subject to eligibility and approval, to eligible staff of Phìtìa Academy (PHICA), approved groups enrolling together, and students who meet the early-payment conditions. The discount applies to tuition only, not registration, exam, materials or certificate charges. Confirm your eligibility and final amount before registering.

How do I pay, and when do I get my certificate?

Before you enroll, admissions will tell you every fee and charge and give you the approved payment schedule for your program. Pay only through official TIME Inc. payment channels and keep every official receipt as proof of payment. Settle all balances within the approved payment period: unpaid balances may block access to exams, certificates, diplomas or other program benefits. Certificates and diplomas are issued only to students who have met all academic, attendance, examination and financial requirements. Any change to fees, dates or payment arrangements is announced by official TIME Inc. notice.

Where are classes held?

At the Phìtìa Academy (PHICA) campus, Slagbaawein Oldfield, Live Smart Community, Tower Hill, behind Christian Aid Ministries, Robertsfield Highway, Margibi County. Get directions.

Who teaches the courses?

Our lecturers hold qualifications ranging from master’s to PhD degrees.

Online learning

Can I study online?

Yes, for the Special Education Support Training Cohort #1. It is a 10-week program delivered 60% online and 40% in person, through the TIME Learning portal.

How do I get into the learning portal?

Create a student account, verify your email address and request enrolment in your course. Once TIME approves your enrolment, your course materials, assignments and class schedule appear in your account. Start at Online Learning.

Phìtìa Academy (PHICA)

What is Phìtìa Academy?

Phìtìa Academy (PHICA) is TIME Inc.’s sister school: a government-registered nursery, elementary and junior high school in Tower Hill, Margibi County, founded to support children whose lives were disrupted by the Ebola crisis. Learn more about PHICA.

How much does PHICA cost?

Many pupils attend tuition-free or receive significant financial help. Families who can contribute pay US$60 or less (Nursery – Grade 6).

How do I enrol my child at PHICA?

Visit the school, call +231 777 891 104, or send us a message about enrollment.

Supporting our work

How can I donate?

See Donate for what your gift provides and how to give from Liberia or abroad. The quickest way to start is to message us on WhatsApp.

What does my donation pay for?

Textbooks, copybooks and pencils for rural schools; benches and chalkboards; scholarships for vulnerable children at Phìtìa Academy; teacher training and salaries; and water and sanitation at PHICA.

Can I give books or supplies instead of money?

Yes. Textbooks, copybooks, pencils, reading books, shoes and school supplies all reach children directly. See what we need.

Can I volunteer?

Yes. You can join outreach trips, teach a class, mentor students, or lend professional skills in health, finance, media or education. Tell us how you would like to help.

Can my organization partner with TIME?

We welcome churches, schools, NGOs, businesses and development partners who want to help scale teacher training and community outreach. Get in touch.
