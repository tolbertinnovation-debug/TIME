# TIME Learning: Terms of Use

**DRAFT for TIME's review. Not yet in force.** Items in [square brackets] are for TIME to decide.

Effective from: [date]

TIME Learning is the online learning platform of the Technical Institute for Many Empowerment (TIME) Inc., Mid-Liberia Christian School System, Ben's Town Community, Marshall Education District, Margibi County, Liberia ("TIME", "we"). These terms are the rules for using it. By creating an account you agree to them.

## 1. Your account

- Give your real, full name. It is printed on your certificate exactly as you enter it.
- Give an email address you can use. It is how you sign in.
- Keep your password secret. Do not share your account or let anyone else study, take a quiz or hand in work in your name.
- Tell TIME at once if you think someone else has used your account.
- One person may hold one account.

## 2. Who may use the platform

- You must be at least [18 years old, or younger with the written consent of a parent or guardian].
- Creating an account does not enrol you. You apply to a class, and the Registrar approves or declines the application.
- Staff accounts are given by TIME's administrators only.

## 3. How you must behave

You agree not to:

- copy another person's work, or hand in work that is not your own;
- share quiz or examination questions or answers;
- post anything abusive, threatening, discriminatory, obscene or unlawful in class discussions or messages;
- report a payment you have not made, or give a false payment reference;
- try to reach another person's account, records or marks, or to interfere with the platform;
- copy, sell or publish TIME's lessons or materials outside the platform.

Lecturers may remove discussion posts that break these rules. Serious or repeated breaches may lead to marks being cancelled, your account being suspended, or removal from your class under TIME's academic rules.

## 4. Learning materials

Lessons and materials belong to TIME or to those who allowed TIME to use them. You may read them, and save them on your own phone for study with no connection, for your own learning only.

Work you hand in remains yours. You allow TIME to store it, mark it, and keep it as part of your academic record.

## 5. Assessment and results

- Each course is marked out of 100 points: continuous assessment 30, assignments and practical activities 20, midterm 20, final 30. The pass mark is 70.
- Written work cannot be handed in after its due date. A timed quiz handed in after its time limit receives no marks. If your connection failed, tell your lecturer, who may allow another attempt.
- Results are final only when approved by TIME's academic leadership and published on the platform.
- You may appeal a published result through the platform within [14 days], giving your reasons. Academic leadership decides the appeal.

## 6. Fees

- Fees for each course are shown before you apply.
- Fees are paid by Mobile Money or bank transfer to the accounts shown on the Fees page, and nowhere else. TIME staff will never ask you to pay into a personal account.
- A payment counts only when TIME's Finance Office has confirmed it. You then receive a numbered receipt.
- Your class opens when your first payment is confirmed. The balance is due 8 weeks after that payment.
- If the balance is unpaid after 8 weeks, your access to the class is paused. It returns when the balance is paid, or when the Finance Office approves a payment arrangement you have requested.
- Refunds: [TIME's refund policy].

## 7. Certificates

- You may request a certificate when TIME has ended the class, your published result is a pass, and your fees are paid in full.
- The Registrar checks your record and academic leadership approves the certificate.
- Anyone can check that a certificate is genuine on the platform's public page, using its number and code. That page shows the holder's name, the award and course, and its date.
- TIME may revoke a certificate that was obtained dishonestly or issued in error. A revoked certificate is shown as revoked when checked.

## 8. Sponsored students

If a sponsor pays for your studies, TIME sends that sponsor a summary of your progress only if you agree on the platform. You may withdraw your agreement at any time. The Privacy Notice explains what the summary contains.

## 9. Withdrawing, deferring and closing your account

- You may ask to withdraw from or defer a class on the platform. The Registrar decides.
- You may ask TIME to close your account. Records TIME must keep, described in the Privacy Notice, are kept after closure.
- TIME may suspend an account that breaks these terms. You may ask TIME's administration to review a suspension.

## 10. Availability

TIME works to keep the platform available but cannot promise it will always be so. Connections fail and maintenance is sometimes needed. Where a failure of the platform itself stops you meeting a deadline, tell your lecturer or use Help, and TIME will treat you fairly.

TIME is not responsible for the cost of your phone, data or connection.

## 11. Changes to these terms

TIME may change these terms. You will be told on the platform before a change takes effect, and asked to accept terms that have changed in an important way.

## 12. Law and contact

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Liberia.

Questions: use **Help** on the platform, or contact TIME at [email address] or [phone number].
