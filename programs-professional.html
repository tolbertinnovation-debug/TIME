Common questions

Is TIME Inc. accredited?

Yes. TIME Inc. is an accredited supplementary teacher-training institution under the Bureau of Teacher Education, Ministry of Education (MoE), Liberia, and has been registered with the Government of Liberia since January 2021.

How much does this program cost?

Tuition is US$125 (LRD 21,250) per course. A registration fee of US$15 may apply, and the exit examination fee is US$10 (LRD 1,700).

What else should I know about fees?

Tuition covers instruction only unless stated in writing; admissions will tell you before you enroll whether learning materials, exams, certificate processing or internship costs are extra. Eligible PHICA staff, approved groups and early payers may receive a 50% tuition discount, subject to approval. All tuition must be paid before the exit examination, and you should pay only through official TIME Inc. channels and keep your receipts.

When are classes held?

Most programs offer a weekday track (Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM) and a Saturday track (8:00 AM – 3:00 PM). Schedules vary by program, so confirm the timetable for your course with admissions.

When does the next class start?

For the 2027 intake, register by Monday, January 4, 2027. Classes begin on Monday, March 8, 2027, and the following intake is on Thursday, October 14, 2027.
