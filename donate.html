What your gift does

Every contribution reaches a classroom

Choose what matters most to you, or let us direct your gift where the need is greatest.

Books & supplies

Textbooks, copybooks, pencils and stationery for children in remote schools in Grand Bassa, Bong and Margibi.

Benches & chalkboards

Replacing damaged chalkboards and repairing benches so children have a proper place to sit and learn.

Scholarships

Keeping a child at Phìtìa Academy in school when their family cannot pay.

Teacher training

Sponsoring a teacher from a rural school to earn a professional certificate. A trained teacher reaches hundreds of children over a career.

Teachers’ pay at PHICA

The founders have paid PHICA teachers from their own salaries. Your gift helps keep qualified teachers in the classroom.

Water & sanitation

Clean water and sanitation facilities for the children and staff of Phìtìa Academy.

Where your support goes

Built on sacrifice, strengthened by partners

For years this work ran without a major donor or grant. The founders gave from their own salaries, and proceeds from the sale of books and educational materials funded outreach.

  • Registered with the Government of Liberia since January 2021
  • Accredited by the Bureau of Teacher Education, Ministry of Education
  • 523 children reached at six remote schools in Grand Bassa County
  • 122 students supported at Kpehpue Town Community School
  • Partner: WE-CARE Liberia
