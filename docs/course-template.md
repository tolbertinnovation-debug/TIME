# Course template for lecturers (10 weeks)

How to lay out a TIME Inc. course in the account workspace (`account.html`, lecturer view) so every course feels the same to students. Based on §1.4 of `docs/online-learning-blueprint.md`. Grade weights and the completion rule are still a leadership decision, so use the weights your program coordinator gives you.

| Week | Post in the workspace | Students do |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Welcome note, course outline, how grading works, your contact hours | Sign in, read the outline, answer the ungraded "platform check" question |
| 1–4 | One module per week: a short reading (text or PDF first), one practice question, a discussion prompt | Read, answer, take part in the discussion |
| 5 | Mid-course check-in message; Assignment 1 | Submit Assignment 1 |
| 6–9 | Modules 5–8 in the same pattern; Assignment 2 | Submit Assignment 2 |
| 10 | Review notes; final assessment; practicum sign-off for teacher programs | Final exam or project |

Longer programs (the 9-month "C" Certificate, the Science Lab program and its internship) repeat the weekly pattern per term.

## Writing for phones and prepaid data

- Text first, PDF second, audio third, short compressed video last.
- Keep each module readable in about 15 minutes on a phone.
- Put every file students need in the module itself, so they can download it once and study offline.
- Record or summarise live sessions for students who miss them.

## Each week

1. Post the module before the week starts.
2. Take attendance for every live or in-person session.
3. Grade submitted work within one week and leave a comment.
4. Message students who have not signed in that week; WhatsApp first, email second.
