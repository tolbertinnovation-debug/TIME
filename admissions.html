Other fees

  • Registration/application fee: US$15, where applicable, in addition to tuition.
  • Exit examination fee: US$10 (LRD 1,700), paid before the examination date. It is separate from tuition.
  • Tuition covers instruction only unless stated in writing. Admissions will tell you before you enroll whether learning materials, exams, certificate processing or internship costs are extra.

50% tuition discount

TIME Inc. may grant a 50% discount on tuition, subject to eligibility and approval, to:

  • Eligible staff of Phìtìa Academy (PHICA)
  • Approved groups enrolling together
  • Students who meet the early-payment conditions

The discount covers tuition only, not registration, exam, materials or certificate charges. Confirm your eligibility before registering.

Payment rules

  • Before you enroll, you’ll be told every fee and charge, and given the approved payment schedule for your program.
  • Pay only through official TIME Inc. payment channels, and keep every official receipt. Mobile Money: 0888 901 848 (Lonestar Cell MTN, name: Philip V. Saywrayne). After paying, send your name, program and the transaction ID to admissions on WhatsApp.
  • Settle all balances within the approved payment period. Unpaid balances may block access to exams, certificates, diplomas or other program benefits.
  • All tuition must be paid, and financial clearance obtained, before the exit examination.
  • Certificates and diplomas are issued only when all academic, attendance, exam and payment requirements are met.
  • Any change to fees, dates or payment arrangements is announced by official TIME Inc. notice.

How to apply

Five steps to start in 2027

  1. 1

    Choose your program

    Compare our programs and courses and pick the one that fits your work and goals.

  2. 2

    Confirm your fees

    Call admissions or message us on WhatsApp to confirm your total amount, timetable and whether you qualify for the 50% tuition discount.

  3. 3

    Register by January 4, 2027

    Register at the Phìtìa Academy campus, Monday – Friday, 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM, and pay the US$15 registration fee where applicable.

  4. 4

    Pay your tuition

    Pay by Mobile Money to 0888 901 848 (Philip V. Saywrayne) or another official TIME Inc. payment channel, and keep every receipt. The “C” Certificate can be paid in two installments.

  5. 5

    Start classes on March 8, 2027

    The administration gives you your exact timetable before classes begin. Pay any balance and the exam fee before the exit examination.
