# Google Business Profile: setup sheet

A free Google Business Profile puts TIME Inc. on Google Maps and in Google Search results, with directions, phone numbers, opening hours and photos. It must be created by someone at TIME Inc. with a Google account, because Google verifies that the business is real.

## 1. Create the profile

1. On a phone or computer, go to **https://business.google.com** and sign in with the Google account that should own the profile (for example `tinstitute312@gmail.com`).
2. Choose **Add your business to Google** and enter the details below.
3. When asked for the location, choose **Yes, I have a location customers can visit**, then **drag the map pin onto the exact spot of the campus**. The address has no street number, so the pin is what customers will follow.
4. Complete **verification**. Google usually asks for a short video showing the signboard, the building and the surrounding road, recorded on site in one take. Follow the on-screen steps. Verification can take a few days.

## 3. After verification

- **Photos:** add the logo, a photo of the signboard and campus entrance, a classroom, and graduation photos (the website photos in `assets/img/` work well).
- **Messaging and phone:** turn on calls; reply to messages and reviews promptly.
- **Posts:** share intake dates and graduation news as Google posts.
- **Reviews:** ask graduates and partners to leave a review.
- **Tell the website maintainer** the profile's Google Maps link and its "Ask for reviews" link, so they can be added to the site's Contact page, footer and structured data.

## 4. Also worth doing

- **Google Search Console** (https://search.google.com/search-console): add the website and submit `sitemap.xml` so Google indexes every page.
- If both TIME Inc. and Phìtìa Academy should appear on Maps, PHICA can have its own profile (primary category: School) at the same location.
