# Guide for academic leadership

For the Dean and the Executive Director. You work from **Administration**, mostly in **Approvals**.

## What waits for your decision

| Request | What to check | What approving does |
|---|---|---|
| **Lessons to publish** | Read the lessons from the link in the request. | Students see the lessons at once. |
| **Results** | Open the class results: marks in every part, totals and passes. | Students see their results and can appeal. |
| **Result appeals** | The student's reason and the marks. | Withdraws that student's published result so the lecturer can re-mark it and send it for approval again. |
| **Certificates** | The registrar has already checked the record. Confirm the name and the award. | The certificate is issued with the next number and can be verified by the public. |

Every decision can be approved or rejected. A rejection needs a short reason, which the person who asked can read.

## What you can see

- **Approvals:** every request on the platform, waiting and decided, including those that belong to the registrar and Finance.
- **Activity:** everything every person has done, with search and filters by person, kind and date.
- **Certificates:** every certificate issued. A certificate can be revoked with a reason; the public check then shows it as revoked.
- **Finance:** balances and payments, to read.
- **Evaluations:** what students said about each class, with the average for each question and their comments. Answers are anonymous, and a class appears once at least three students have answered.
- **Reports:** enrolment, results and payments, downloaded as spreadsheets for one intake or all of them.
- **Transcripts:** any student's transcript, from **Accounts**.

## The certificate itself

It follows TIME's paper certificate. The platform prints the wording, the holder's name, the date and the number. The three signature lines are left blank and are signed by hand after printing. No seal or signature is printed by the platform.
