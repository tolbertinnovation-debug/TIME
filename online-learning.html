TIME Learning

How online learning works

Study with TIME from home, at work or on campus. Lessons are built for a phone and prepaid data, and your lecturer and our staff are with you from the first week to graduation.

Three ways to study

On campus, blended or online

Each course is taught in one of three ways. Special Education Support Training is already blended (60% online, 40% in person). For other courses the mode shown in the catalogue is planned, and admissions confirms it before each intake.

On campus

Classes at the Margibi campus

You attend classes in person, as TIME has always taught. Notes, handouts and assignments are also posted in TIME Learning, so you can catch up if you miss a day.

Blended

Online lessons plus in-person sessions

You study weekly lessons online and meet your lecturer in person for practical work, discussion and assessment. Most courses are planned this way.

Online

Fully online

Lessons, live classes, assignments and the final assessment all happen online. Planned for courses such as QuickBooks, Monitoring & Evaluation and Research Skills.

See the planned mode for every course in Browse by school.

A course at a glance

What 10 weeks look like

Most courses follow the same simple rhythm, so you always know what comes next.

  1. Week 0

    Orientation: sign in, find your course outline and meet your lecturer.

  2. Weeks 1–4

    The first modules, each with a short reading, a practice quiz and a discussion.

  3. Week 5

    A mid-course check-in with your lecturer and your first assignment.

  4. Weeks 6–9

    The remaining modules and a second assignment that applies what you learned.

  5. Week 10

    Review and the final assessment. Teacher programs also complete the practicum sign-off.

The “C” Certificate runs for 9 months and the Science Lab program adds a 2-month internship. Your lecturer shares the exact plan and how your work is graded in week 0.

What you need

A phone and a little data is enough

  • A smartphone, tablet or computer with a recent web browser.
  • Prepaid data. Lessons are text first, so they load quickly and cost little.
  • Study offline. Pages you have opened stay available, and course notes can be downloaded.
  • WhatsApp. We send reminders and answer questions there first.

Try it before you apply

TIME Learning has free short courses you can open right now, with no account. They work just like a real course: units, practice questions and notes.

Your journey

From application to graduation

  1. Apply online or on WhatsApp

    Choose a course, an intake and a class schedule. The application form takes about two minutes.

  2. Admissions contacts you

    We check your details, answer your questions and confirm your place, usually within a few working days.

  3. Pay your tuition

    Pay in full, or the first installment where your program allows two. The second installment is due before the exit exam. See fees and payment.

  4. Get your TIME Learning account

    You receive your sign-in and are added to your class.

  5. Learn, submit work and take part

    Follow the weekly lessons, submit assignments, and see your grades and attendance in your account.

  6. Final assessment and graduation

    Complete the final assessment. Your results are approved by the academic team and you receive your TIME Inc. certificate.

Questions

Online learning FAQ

Is an online TIME course the same as a campus course?

Yes. It covers the same content and leads to the same TIME Inc. certificate. Only the way you attend is different.

What if my network is slow?

Lessons are mostly text and load on slow connections. Open a lesson when you have signal and it stays available offline. If you miss a live session, ask your lecturer for the notes.

How do I sign in?

After you enrol, admissions creates your account. Use Student & lecturer sign-in with the details you receive.

How can an employer check my certificate?

They can use our certificate check page and our records office will confirm it.
