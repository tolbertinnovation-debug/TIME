Who we are

Born in a classroom, built on compassion.

The Technical Institute for Many Empowerment (TIME) Inc. began when a teacher stopped to ask why his students were falling behind and listened to the answers.

Behind the missed assignments and empty seats were stories of poverty, survival and a deep desire to stay in school. Dr. Philip V. Saywrayne, III and his wife, Mrs. Tibeh E. Gbassagee-Saywrayne, began helping quietly from their own limited resources. In January 2021 that personal commitment became TIME Inc.: a registered Liberian institution for teacher education, skills training and humanitarian service.

Today we are an accredited supplementary teacher-training institution under the Bureau of Teacher Education, Ministry of Education, and we still go on foot when that's what it takes to reach a village school.

Our sister school

Phìtìa Academy (PHICA)

A government-registered elementary and junior high school in Tower Hill, Margibi County, founded for children whose lives were upended by the Ebola crisis.

What began as an open field of savanna grass is now a place where children hold books, learn to read and write, and imagine a different future. Many attend tuition-free.
