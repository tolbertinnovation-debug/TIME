About Dr. Saywrayne

A lifelong commitment to quality education

Throughout his career, Dr. Saywrayne has worked to advance quality education, strengthen educational leadership and empower underserved communities through capacity-building.

As Executive Director of TIME Inc., he leads teacher education, vocational and technical training, and outreach to underprivileged women, children and other vulnerable people in Bong, Grand Bassa and Margibi Counties.

He has taught undergraduate and graduate students in Research Methodology, Education, English, Sociology and Educational Leadership at the University of Liberia, Carver Christian University, Cuttington University and Trinity University Graduate School, and has mentored many students, emerging scholars and school leaders.
